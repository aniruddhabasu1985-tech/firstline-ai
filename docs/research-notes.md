# Research Notes

Market context behind the design (September 2026):

- **Deflection vs resolution:** Gartner research finds AI deflects more than 45% of customer queries while only ~14% reach true self-service resolution — the gap that motivates measuring *resolution quality* (FDR, repeat asks, status accuracy) rather than activity.
- **The rebalancing lesson:** several high-profile full-automation customer service rollouts in 2024–2026 later rehired human agents for complex cases. The industry's takeaway is hybrid: AI handles routine work and prepares humans for complex work — the posture FirstLine takes by design.
- **Enterprise agentic platforms** (major consultancies and CX vendors) converge on multi-agent orchestration with an explicit governance layer rather than one general assistant.
- **Outcome-based pricing** (per-resolution pricing in the ~$1–$2 range for AI-resolved tickets) is replacing seat licensing in AI customer service — the same shift that makes per-case and value-share outsourcing models worth modeling.
- **Model pricing** was taken from vendor pricing pages and independent trackers in September 2026 (OpenAI, Google Gemini API, Anthropic). Promotional rates were replaced with list rates in the cost model.
- **Governance frameworks referenced:** NIST AI Risk Management Framework; ISO/IEC 42001; EU AI Act transparency obligations.
